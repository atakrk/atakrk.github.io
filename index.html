German job market pipeline

I wanted to know which tools German data engineering roles actually ask for, so I measured it instead of guessing. Two public job APIs into Databricks, modelled with dbt, deduplicated across sources and rebuilt every morning. Azure runs about 1.4× AWS — the reverse of the global picture — and roughly two in five postings state an explicit German-language requirement. The exact figures are on the dashboard, and they move.
